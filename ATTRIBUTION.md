# Attribution

Photo and data sources for the Porto City Break map.

## Map tiles

- **Esri World Street Map / Topographic / Imagery** — default base layers, used without an API key under Esri's public tile terms. Attribution is rendered on the map: *Tiles © Esri — Esri, HERE, Garmin, OpenStreetMap contributors*.
- **OpenStreetMap** — optional layer, only enabled when the page is served over http(s). © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Volunteer tile servers — personal use only, do not hammer them.

## Not included

- **Star ratings.** No free source exists; Google Places, Foursquare and Yelp all require a key. Nothing was invented.
- **3 destinations** could not be placed reliably by the geocoder and are listed in the sidebar but not pinned: *Vodafone Building*, *Sta. Catarina Chapel Viewpoint*, *Infante Bridge*.
- Photos for buildings with no free image on Commons (modern office blocks, minor gardens, walls).
